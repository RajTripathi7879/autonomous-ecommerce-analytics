# Business Insights

## 1. Strong Sales Growth

Sales showed a slight decline in 2015 but experienced strong and consistent growth from 2015 to 2017, reaching the highest level in 2017. Profit followed a similar upward trend, indicating improving overall business performance.

## 2. Regional Sales Performance

The West region generated the highest sales at $0.73M, followed by the East at $0.68M. The Central region generated $0.50M, while the South recorded the lowest sales at $0.39M. This indicates that the West and East regions are the strongest contributors to overall sales.

## 3. Profit by Category

Technology is the most profitable category, generating approximately $145K in profit, followed by Office Supplies at $122K. Furniture generates only around $18K in profit, significantly lower than the other categories. This indicates that Technology and Office Supplies are the primary contributors to overall profitability.

## 4. West Region Has the Highest Profit Margin

The West region has the highest profit margin at 14.94%, followed by the East at 13.48% and South at 11.93%. The Central region has the lowest profit margin at 7.92%. This indicates that the West region is not only the strongest in sales but also operates with the highest profitability relative to its sales.

## 5. Higher Discounts Are Associated with Lower Profitability

The analysis shows that profit is generally concentrated around lower discount levels, while higher discount levels are associated with lower or negative profit values. Several products with higher discounts show losses, suggesting that excessive discounting can reduce profitability. However, the relationship is not perfectly linear, indicating that other factors also influence product profitability.

## 6. Specific Products Are Driving Significant Losses

The loss-making products analysis shows that a small number of products contribute substantial negative profit. The Cubify CubeX 3D Printer Double Head Print records the largest loss at approximately $8,879.97, followed by the Lexmark MX611dhe Monochrome Laser Printer at approximately $4,589.97. These products should be reviewed for pricing, discount levels, costs, and overall sales performance.

## 7. Business Recommendations

### 1. Review Discounting Strategies

The business should review discount levels, particularly for products where higher discounts are associated with low or negative profit. Setting appropriate discount limits and evaluating profitability before offering large discounts can help protect profit margins while maintaining competitive pricing.

### 2. Review Loss-Making Products

The business should investigate products generating significant negative profit, particularly the products with the largest losses. Management should review their pricing, discount levels, costs, and sales performance to determine whether corrective action, repricing, or changes in the discount strategy are required.

### 3. Focus on Lower-Performing Regions

The business should investigate the lower sales and profitability performance of the South and Central regions. Regional strategies such as targeted promotions, product assortment adjustments, and improved sales initiatives could be used to strengthen performance while maintaining the profitability levels achieved in stronger regions.

### 4. Strengthen High-Performing Categories

The business should continue investing in Technology and Office Supplies, which are the strongest contributors to overall profit. Product availability, targeted promotions, and inventory planning for these categories should be prioritized to sustain their strong performance.

### 5. Sustain Recent Growth

The business should examine the factors that contributed to the strong sales and profit growth observed in 2016 and 2017. Successful products, regions, and sales strategies identified during this period can be evaluated and replicated where appropriate to support continued growth.
